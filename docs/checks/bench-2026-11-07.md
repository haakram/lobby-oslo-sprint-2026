# Benchmark and offline tune, 2026-11-07

Offline half only. **The live validation (`bench --seeds 1-3 --live`) has NOT been run yet.**

## Offline tune

Command: `python -m agent tune --seeds 1-20 --trials 30` (about 2 min real time). Generated nights from
`agent.bench.offline_scenario(seed)` on the fake server, 22:00 to 02:00 sim, random search (rng seed 0),
statistic = mean with the worst night dropped (as in the final ranking).

| Params | mean | mean-drop-worst |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline (`Params()` defaults) | 687.6 | 693.7 |
| Best found (trial 2) | 690.8 | 696.5 |

Best params (committed as `agent/data/params_best.json`): `cart_stock` charger 4, pillow 4, toiletry 1,
towel 3, water 2; `decay_per_min` 0.71; `max_scans` 3; `use_cart` true.

Read this honestly: the gain is +2.8 (0.4 %) and the trials spread between 658 and 697, so the best is
inside the noise of a 20-night sample. The loser group (about 650) is the one with `use_cart` false.
The search says: keep the cart, other settings barely matter on the fake. It is a sanity check, not a
proven improvement. Real tuning needs the live validation.
